# 0.2: Resources for the polythings projects

**In progress**

The first release as a resources library: `Clock`, `Random`, `Env`, `Console`, and `Store` with test, system, and
JavaScript implementations, the platforms, and the macros for new resources and bundles. Enough for lakekit to take
its store, clock, and randomness from loko instead of defining them, and for Timelake and lakeglass to follow.
